# Content Moderation

This document describes the content moderation criteria and process for Lernza quests.

## Reportable Categories

Quests can be reported for the following reasons:

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Spam** | Repeated, irrelevant, or automated content that does not represent a genuine learning quest. |
| **Misleading** | Quest descriptions or reward promises that are inaccurate or deceptive. |
| **Offensive** | Content containing hate speech, slurs, or harassment targeting individuals or groups. |
| **Scam** | Quests designed to defraud participants, including phishing, wallet-draining links, or fake rewards. |
| **Inappropriate** | Explicit, violent, or otherwise objectionable language or imagery. |
| **Other** | Violations of the terms of service not covered by the above categories. |

## Moderation Process

1. **Report submission** - A user submits a report via the "Report this quest" button on the quest view page. The report includes the selected reason and optional free-text details.

2. **Queue review** - Reports enter a moderation queue. Each report is reviewed by a human moderator.

3. **Action** - Depending on severity and evidence, the moderator may:
   - Dismiss the report (no violation found).
   - Issue a warning to the quest creator.
   - Hide the quest from public listings.
   - Archive or cancel the quest.

4. **Appeal** - Quest creators may appeal moderation decisions by contacting the team with supporting evidence.

## Severity Levels

- **Low** - Minor policy violations, such as mildly inappropriate language. Warning issued.
- **Medium** - Misleading descriptions, spam campaigns, or repeated violations. Quest hidden pending review.
- **High** - Scams, offensive content, or phishing attempts. Quest immediately archived and creator flagged.

## Automated Checks

Future versions may include automated detection for:
- Keyword filtering in quest titles and descriptions.
- Token address validation against known scam lists.
- Rate limiting on quest creation to prevent spam.
